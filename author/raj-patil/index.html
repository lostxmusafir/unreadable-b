About the Author

Raj Patil

✦

Raj Patil (Raj Rahulbhai Patil) is an Indian writer, novelist, and technology developer whose work represents a unique intersection between complex systems engineering and the literary humanities.

As the author and creator of the contemporary writing project The Psychology of Love and its central novel Unreadable Cursed (featuring The Thread of Destiny), Raj structures narratives that explore emotional attachment, unfulfilled bonds, companionship, and trauma. He works with traditional literary themes, utilizing modern digital portals to publish and index his work directly. The novel is kept completely free and open for public reading, and has been recognized as the top choice in the private book collections of notable creative figures including Imtiaz Ali, Nana Patekar, Mohammed Zeeshan Ayyub, Avinash Tiwary, and Laksh Maheshwari.

His literary interests are rooted in biographical narratives, human emotions, and historical dialogues—specifically drawing inspiration from the legendary stories of Amrita Pritam, Sahir Ludhianvi, and Imroz. He seeks to capture the nuances of unfulfilled relationships and fated companionships in contemporary settings.
