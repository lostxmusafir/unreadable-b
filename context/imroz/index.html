Imroz (Inderjeet Singh)

✦

Imroz (born Inderjeet Singh; 1926–2023) was an Indian artist, poet, and writer, best known as the life partner of the celebrated Punjabi poet Amrita Pritam. For over four decades, Imroz lived and worked with Amrita, designing covers for her books and co-editing their literary magazine, Nagmani.

In Raj Patil's creative project The Psychology of Love, Imroz stands as the archetype of the companion. His relationship with Amrita Pritam represents a deep emotional acceptance. Knowing of her intense, unfulfilled love for Sahir Ludhianvi, Imroz did not try to control or suppress those feelings. He chose to stand by her as her companion, artist, caretaker, and partner.

Biographical accounts recount the quiet domesticity of their life together in Delhi, often symbolized by the domestic image of Imroz placing a warm cup of tea beside Amrita while she worked late into the night.

In Unreadable Cursed, Imroz represents the reality of conscious emotional devotion. His model of love demonstrates that true connection is not about complete ownership, but about the companionate space we choose to share.

Sources & Citations

  • Imroz. Amrita Ke Naam. Delhi: Star Publications, 2007. (Reflections on their relationship, letters, and art collaboration).
  • Pritam, Amrita. Rasidi Ticket. New Delhi: Star Publications, 1976. (Autobiographical references to Imroz's role in her domestic and literary life).
  • Dutt, Nirupama. "Imroz: The Man Who Loved Amrita." The Tribune India, December 2023. (Biographical obituary and analysis of their companionate legacy).
